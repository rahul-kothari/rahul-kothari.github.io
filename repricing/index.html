EIP-8037 gas reservoir

Glamsterdam · EIP-8037

Repricing of state gas vs execution gas

  • EIP-8037 splits a transaction's gas into two pools: execution gas and the reservoir (state gas).
  • Execution gas is capped to 16.7M, but total tx.gas is now 4.2B. Anything outside the 16.7M goes into the reservoir.
  • Everything is execution gas except creation of state. (See What pays from which pool.)
  • If state creation is undone, the gas refund goes back to the pool where it came from.
  • 8037 also makes new state far more expensive.
  • gasleft() only sees execution gas left, not all the gas. This breaks a ton of assumptions.

The rules behind it

  1. At the start, up to 16,777,216 of tx.gas (after intrinsic gas) becomes gas_left. The rest goes into the reservoir.
  2. Execution gas is paid from gas_left only.
  3. State gas is paid from the reservoir first, then from gas_left.
  4. When a state creation is undone, its gas is refilled last in, first out: back into gas_left first, up to what was taken from it.
  5. gasleft() returns gas_left only. The 63/64 rule applies to gas_left only; a callee borrows the whole reservoir.
